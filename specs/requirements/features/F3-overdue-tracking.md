# Overdue Tracking

## Purpose

Give the office manager a clear view of what equipment is currently out and which loans are overdue.

Needs: F2.

## User Stories

- F3.1 As an office manager, I see a list of all equipment currently out, with who has it and when it is due back.
- F3.2 As an office manager, I see which of those loans are overdue (past their due date).
- F3.3 As an office manager, I mark any loan as returned, in case the borrower does not.
- F3.4 As an office manager, I extend a loan's due date.

## Decisions

- The office manager's view covers current and overdue loans only, not the full loan history.

## Out of Scope

- A full loan history view for the office manager (past, already-returned loans).
